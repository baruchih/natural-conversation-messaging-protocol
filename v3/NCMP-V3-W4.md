# NCMP-V3-W4

**Status:** V3 Experimental Result #15 — YES, frozen\
**Date:** August 2026\
**Parent:** HK2 (Result #14, frozen). Connects to W1, W2, W3.\
**Scope:** Surface distinguishability of HK2 conversations
generated by the existing deterministic P7-family
encoders. Classification unit is the 4-turn conversation.
Corpus first. There is no HK3.

This profile is closed. Do not retune E to a detector.
W4 does not test LM-proposed next turns. That is M1.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1. Question

> Are HK2 conversations generated by the existing
> deterministic P7-family encoders distinguishable?

Not: can an LM-generated natural conversation be
constrained into a valid HK2 stream.

NB is 1.00 because the corpus is the old encoder family.

restaurant was good but service was sluggish around

a proof that rolling NCMP must have one.

is not camouflage.
